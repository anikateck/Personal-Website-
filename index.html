About Me

Hi! My name is Anika, and I'm a student at Northeastern University studying Finance and Data Science. I've always enjoyed meeting new people, taking on new experiences, and getting involved in activities that allow me to grow both personally and professionally. Whether through school, work, or volunteering, I value opportunities to connect with others and learn something new.

Academically, I'm particularly interested in finance and hope to pursue a career in private equity or investment management. Through my coursework and professional experiences, I've had the opportunity to explore different areas of the finance industry and develop a better understanding of what I want to do in the future.

Beyond academics and my career interests, community service has been one of the most consistent parts of my life. My involvement began in eighth grade when I was selected as an ambassador for Project 351, a Massachusetts nonprofit that brings together young leaders from across the state through service. Since then, I've continued volunteering through different organizations and initiatives, and I remain involved with Project 351 as an alum. What I enjoy most about service is the opportunity to work alongside people from different backgrounds who share a commitment to helping others.

Outside of these commitments, I enjoy public speaking, Bollywood dance, watching TV thrillers, traveling, and spending time with friends and family.

I created this website to share more about my community service journey, the organizations I've worked with, and the experiences that have made volunteering such an important part of my life.
