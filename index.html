About Me

Hi! My name is Anika, and I'm a student at Northeastern University studying Finance and Data Science. Outside of academics, one of my biggest passions is community service and finding ways to give back. Volunteering has been an important part of my life, and I enjoy getting involved in opportunities that make a difference.

Through Project 351, I've had the chance to participate in service initiatives and connect with other young leaders across Massachusetts. Being involved in Project 351 has helped me develop my leadership skills, meet new people, and understand the importance of working together to support others.

Some of my favorite experiences have involved participating in community service events, including the 9/11 Tribute Service and WBZ Service Day. I enjoy seeing people come together for a common purpose and knowing that even small actions can have an impact.

At Northeastern, I also enjoy being involved in mentorship programs and helping other students feel supported. Outside of volunteering, I enjoy public speaking, Bollywood dance, spending time with friends and family, and watching TV shows.

I created this website to share more about myself and my experiences with community service. Volunteering has introduced me to so many people and opportunities, and I hope to continue staying involved in service throughout college and beyond.
